Who we are

A channel partner that sits on your side.

BiznorX Realty is the Mumbai real estate arm of the BiznorX group. We work with developers as their channel partner — appointed to market and sell their projects — and we focus on new and pre-launch homes, with commercial spaces alongside.

For you, that means official price sheets, launch offers and the developer’s own paperwork, with one advisor from your first call to the day you get your keys. If a project isn’t right for you, we’ll say so.
