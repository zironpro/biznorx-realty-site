What is the pre-launch offer?

You can reserve your home in The Aura with an Expression of Interest (EOI) of ₹50,000. The EOI is fully refundable. When the project launches you choose your unit at pre-launch terms and move to booking and agreement.

Who is developing The Aura?

The Aura is developed by SAK Buildcon & TrueNest Developers under Al Mantasha Realty — the group behind The Universe in Mumbra.

Where exactly is it?

In Kausa, Mumbra, near Kalsekar Hospital & College. Sales office: Survey No. 110/1/A & 110/1/B, near Kalsekar Hospital & College, Kausa, Mumbra, Thane 400612.

What sizes are available?

1, 2, 3, 4 and 5 bedroom residences across three 22-floor towers and a 7-floor building, plus bungalows and shops for retail and offices. Ask us for the floor plans with exact areas.

Is BiznorX Realty authorised to sell The Aura?

Yes. BiznorX Realty is an authorised channel partner for The Aura, working directly with Al Mantasha Realty. Your EOI and all payments go to the developer, against the developer’s receipt.

I live outside India. Can I still reserve?

Yes. We do video walkthroughs, share every document digitally and guide you through the EOI, agreement and registration from the UAE or anywhere else.
